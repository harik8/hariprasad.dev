About

Hi, I'm Hari — a Senior Cloud, DevOps, and Platform Engineer with over 10 years of experience designing and operating scalable, cloud-native platforms and developer-focused infrastructure. I specialize in AWS and DevOps practices, with a strong focus on building reliable, scalable, and automated solutions that enable teams to deliver efficiently and confidently. Throughout my career, I've been driven by a passion for solving complex problems and continuously improving the way systems are designed, deployed, and operated.

Since 2021, I've been an active member of the AWS Community Builders program, where I share my knowledge and experiences through articles, videos, and community contributions. I enjoy learning new technologies, exchanging ideas, and helping others navigate their cloud journeys.

Outside of work, you'll often find me biking, going for long walks, and exploring photography.
